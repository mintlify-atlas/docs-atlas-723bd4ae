## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search the CVR registry">
    Use the company search page to filter Danish companies by industry code, location, employee count, company type, and founding date. Save any company to your watchlist with a single click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up lead triggers">
    Create a trigger with your target filters and a schedule (daily or weekly). CVR-MATE automatically runs the query and notifies you of new matching companies — no manual checking required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enrich with AI">
    Open any company profile and generate an AI briefing powered by Google Gemini. Get a company summary, key insights, and a suggested outreach approach tailored to your brand profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach out and track">
    Draft personalized emails, LinkedIn messages, or phone scripts using AI. Create todos linked to companies, sync leads to your CRM, and track all activity in the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
