CVR-MATE integrates Google Gemini to automate the research and outreach work that would otherwise take hours per company. Instead of manually reading a company's registry data and crafting a bespoke message, you can generate a structured briefing, a tailored email, or a LinkedIn script in seconds — all grounded in live CVR data and personalized to your brand.

## AI features

### Company briefing

Available on every company profile, the **briefing** generator produces:

- A prose summary of the company's business, size, and market position
- A list of **key insights** derived from the company's industry code, employee history, financial data, and registered participants
- A **suggested outreach approach** specific to the company's profile

Briefings are persisted so they load instantly on repeat visits. Each briefing is stored in `company_briefing` and linked to your user account and the company's VAT number.

### Pipeline analysis

The **pipeline analysis** feature reads across all your saved companies and open todos and produces a holistic assessment of your pipeline health: which segments are over-represented, which companies have been idle too long, and what to prioritize next. Access it from your dashboard.

### Natural language search

Instead of manually filling in filter fields, describe the companies you want in plain English or Danish:

```
"Tech startups in Aarhus with fewer than 20 employees founded after 2020"
```

CVR-MATE sends the query to Gemini, which parses it into structured `SearchCompanyParams` (industry code, city, employee range, founding date) and pre-fills the search filters for you. The endpoint is `/api/ai/parse-search`.

### Todo suggestions

On any company profile, click **Suggest next steps** to get a ranked list of AI-generated action items based on the company's data — for example, "Request intro via mutual connection at [participant name]" or "Verify revenue trend before proposal." Suggestions are generated by `/api/ai/suggest-todos`.

### Outreach drafting

Generate ready-to-send outreach for a specific company in three formats:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Email">
    Full email with subject line, body, and a follow-up suggestion. Formal or casual tone.
  </Card>
  <Card title="LinkedIn message">
    A concise connection request or direct message optimized for LinkedIn character limits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Phone script">
    A structured call guide with an opener, key talking points, and objection handling.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

All three formats support **formal** and **casual** tone. The generated draft is persisted in `outreach_message` so you can retrieve it later without regenerating.

### Company enrichment

The `/api/ai/enrich-company` endpoint generates an AI intelligence layer on top of raw CVR data: inferred market positioning, growth signals, competitive context, and strategic notes. Enrichment data is stored in `profile_enrichment` (entity type `company`) and shown on the company profile.

### Person enrichment

Similarly, `/api/ai/enrich-person` enriches a participant profile with AI-inferred context: career trajectory, seniority signals, and relationship-building notes based on their registered roles and company affiliations.

## Usage limits

AI features consume from a per-month quota tracked against your plan.

| Plan | AI usages per month |
|---|---|
| Free | 5 |
| Starter | 10 |
| Professional | 50 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

When you exhaust your monthly quota, AI endpoints return a rate-limit error. Quotas reset at the start of each calendar month.

<Note>
CVR-MATE uses **gemini-2.5-flash** as the default model for all AI features. **gemini-2.5-pro** and **gemini-2.0-flash-lite** are also supported internally for different quality and cost trade-offs.
</Note>

## Brand personalization

All AI output is personalized using the brand profile you set up during onboarding. The profile includes:

| Field | Effect on AI output |
|---|---|
| **Company name** | Referenced in outreach as the sender company |
| **Products / services** | Contextualized in value propositions and briefings |
| **Target audience** | Used to calibrate which CVR segments are highlighted as relevant |
| **Tone** | `formal` or `casual` — sets the default voice for all generated content |

<Tip>
Complete your brand profile at `/onboarding` (or update it in `/settings`) before generating your first outreach draft. Without a brand profile, AI output falls back to generic templates that do not mention your company or products.
</Tip>
