This page walks you through everything you need to go from a blank account to running your first CVR company search and having an automated lead trigger in place. If you are self-hosting CVR-MATE, there is a section at the end covering local setup.

<Info>
  The hosted version of CVR-MATE requires no local installation. If you are using the hosted app, skip directly to step 1.
</Info>

## Prerequisites

If you are self-hosting, you need the following before you start:

- **Node.js** LTS (v20 or later recommended)
- **pnpm** package manager
- **PostgreSQL** database (v14 or later)

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Navigate to `/signup` and register with your email address and a password of at least 8 characters, or sign in with Google OAuth for immediate access.

    After signing up with email and password, CVR-MATE sends a verification email to the address you provided. Click the link in that email to verify your account — login is blocked until verification is complete. Once you click the link, you are signed in automatically.

    <Tip>
      If you do not receive the verification email within a few minutes, try signing in again. CVR-MATE automatically resends the verification email when an unverified account attempts to log in.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    After your first successful login, you are redirected to `/onboarding`. This one-time setup screen collects your **brand profile**: your company name, industry, and a short description of your ideal customer. CVR-MATE uses this profile to personalize AI-generated briefings and outreach drafts so they reflect your voice and positioning.

    Complete all fields and click **Finish setup** to reach your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your first CVR search">
    Go to `/search`. The search page queries the Danish CVR registry in real time. Use the filter panel to narrow results by:

    - **Industry code (branchekode)** — filter by one or more NACE industry codes
    - **Municipality** — narrow by Danish municipality or region
    - **Company type** — e.g. A/S, ApS, I/S
    - **Employee count** — set a minimum and maximum headcount range
    - **Founded between** — restrict results to companies founded within a date range

    You can also type a natural-language description into the search bar — the AI search parser converts your description into structured filters automatically.

    Click any result to open the full company profile at `/company/[vat]`. From the profile you can save the company, create todos, push to your CRM, or generate an AI briefing.

    <Tip>
      Click **Save search** on any filtered query to store it as a reusable saved search at `/saved-searches`. You can rerun it at any time or turn it into a lead trigger.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a company and create a lead trigger">
    On any company profile, click **Save** to add the company to your watchlist at `/saved`. Saved companies are tracked across sessions and count toward your plan's saved-company limit (5 on Free, 25 on Starter, 100 on Professional, unlimited on Enterprise).

    To automate prospecting, go to `/triggers` and click **New trigger**. Choose your target filters — the same options available in the search page — and set a schedule (daily or weekly). CVR-MATE runs the query on that schedule and sends you an in-app notification whenever new matching companies appear in the registry.

    <Info>
      The Free plan allows 1 active trigger. Upgrade to Starter (3 triggers), Professional (5 triggers), or Enterprise (unlimited) to run more concurrent monitors.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate an AI briefing">
    Open any saved company profile and click **Generate briefing**. CVR-MATE calls the Google Gemini API and returns:

    - A concise company summary drawn from registry data
    - Key business insights relevant to your sales context
    - A suggested outreach approach personalized to your brand profile

    You can also generate a **draft outreach email**, a **LinkedIn message**, or a **phone script** from the same profile. Each draft is personalized using your brand profile from onboarding and can be edited before copying it to your outreach tool.

    <Note>
      AI usage is metered per month. The Free plan includes 5 AI uses per month. Starter gives you 10, Professional gives you 50, and Enterprise is unlimited.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Self-hosted setup

If you are running CVR-MATE on your own infrastructure, follow these steps after cloning the repository.

1. Copy the environment variables file and fill in your values:

```bash
cp .env.example .env.local
```

2. Install dependencies, push the database schema, and start the development server:

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm db:push
pnpm dev
```

The app runs at `http://localhost:3000`.

For a full list of required and optional environment variables, see [Environment variables reference](/environment-variables).

<Tip>
  Use `pnpm db:studio` to open Drizzle Studio, a browser-based UI for inspecting and editing your database tables during development.
</Tip>
