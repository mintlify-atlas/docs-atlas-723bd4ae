---
title: "Team roles and permissions: Owner, Admin, Member"
description: "CVR-MATE teams use three roles — Owner, Admin, and Member — with distinct permissions controlling who can invite, remove, and edit shared team resources."
---

CVR-MATE teams use three roles: **Owner**, **Admin**, and **Member**. Every member of an organization holds exactly one role at a time. Roles determine who can invite, remove, or manage other members, and who can edit shared resources created by colleagues.

## Role descriptions

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Owner" icon="crown">
    Full control over the organization. Can invite and remove any member, change roles, transfer ownership, rename the org, and delete it. There is always exactly one owner per organization.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield">
    Can invite members and other admins, remove members with a lower role rank, and rename the organization. Cannot change roles, transfer ownership, or delete the org.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Member" icon="user">
    Can view all shared team resources and create or edit their own resources. Cannot invite or remove others.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Permissions table

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
|--------|:-----:|:-----:|:------:|
| Create org | ✓ | — | — |
| Invite member / admin | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Remove member | ✓ | ✓* | — |
| Remove admin | ✓ | — | — |
| Change role | ✓ | — | — |
| Transfer ownership | ✓ | — | — |
| Rename org | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Delete org | ✓ | — | — |
| Leave org | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| View team resources | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Edit / delete own resources | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Edit / delete others' resources | ✓ | ✓ | — |

\* Admins can only remove members whose role rank is lower than their own.

## Role rank hierarchy

Roles have a numeric rank used to enforce the rule above:

| Role | Rank |
|------|:----:|
| Owner | 3 |
| Admin | 2 |
| Member | 1 |

<Note>
  An actor can only act on members with a **strictly lower** rank. An admin (rank 2) can remove a member (rank 1) but cannot remove another admin (rank 2) or the owner (rank 3). This is enforced server-side on every remove and role-change request.
</Note>

## Resource-level authorization

Being a member of an organization does not grant blanket write access to everything in it. Resource-level rules apply on top of role permissions:

- **Members** can only edit or delete resources they personally created, even if those resources are in team scope.
- **Admins and owners** can edit or delete any resource that belongs to the team.

Personal-scope resources (those created outside of team scope) are always private — no team member can access them regardless of role.

## Transferring ownership

The owner can transfer their role to any existing member of the organization.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team settings">
    Go to **Settings → Team** and find the member you want to make the new owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate transfer">
    Click **Transfer ownership** and select the target member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Read the confirmation prompt — "You will become an admin after this transfer" — and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the transfer:
- The selected member's role changes to **Owner**.
- Your role changes to **Admin**.
- The change is recorded in the audit log as `ownership_transferred`.

You cannot transfer ownership to yourself, and the target must already be a member of the organization.

<Tip>
  Assign the **Admin** role to team leads who need to manage day-to-day membership (inviting new hires, removing departed colleagues) without granting them the ability to delete the organization or reassign roles broadly. Reserve **Owner** for the account holder or primary decision-maker.
</Tip>
